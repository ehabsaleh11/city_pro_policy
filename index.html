Bro City: Street Fight Game — Privacy Policy

Local game data

The game stores progress, settings, virtual currency, unlocked content and a generated online nickname on your device. Offline play does not require online sign-in. The current free edition has no advertisements or real-money purchases. Operating-system backups may retain or restore local data.

Optional online play

When you choose online play, the game signs in with a pseudonymous Unity Authentication identity. You do not enter an email address, password or real name. Unity Gaming Services provides Authentication, multiplayer sessions/Lobby and Relay. These services process player identifiers, IP addresses, connection and session information, approximate location derived from connection information, and service activity/performance information to operate, secure and maintain online play.

A generated nickname, selected character, session participation and gameplay state are transmitted to Unity and other participants as needed for multiplayer. Private rooms use an invitation code; anyone you give the code to may join while space is available. Quick Match uses public rooms and may connect you with people you do not know. This edition has no free-text player names, text chat, voice chat or user-uploaded images. Do not send private information through support unless needed for your request.

Unity processes data under its applicable terms and privacy notices:
https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Online service connections use HTTPS/WSS or encrypted DTLS. We do not sell player information or use it for advertising in this edition. Providers may process information internationally. No security measure is absolute.

Online availability settings

The game checks Firebase Remote Config for online availability and maintenance messages. These requests can occur at startup, even before choosing online play. Firebase processes an installation identifier and connection information to deliver these settings. The last settings are stored on your device. Google Analytics collection is disabled in this edition. See Firebase privacy information.

Support and requests to delete online data

To request deletion of your Bro City online identity and associated online data, email developera44@gmail.com with the subject "Bro City - online data deletion request". Include your online player ID if available. In the game, open Settings > Online Data & Deletion to copy the ID or prepare the email. You must send the email yourself; opening the email draft does not submit a request or delete data.

You may also request deletion by emailing us directly without reinstalling the game. If you no longer have your player ID, explain that in your request; we will help identify the relevant record and may request limited information to verify ownership. A player ID alone is not sufficient proof of ownership. Never send passwords or authentication/session tokens.

We use request correspondence to verify and fulfil the request. We arrange removal of the Authentication identity and associated online information, including requests to Unity where a service requires provider assistance. We will explain the expected timing, confirm the outcome and describe any information retained for legitimate security or legal reasons. Deleting an identity alone does not automatically erase every provider record. Relay documents a normal personal-data retention period of 30 days; other services apply their own retention and backup rules.

Local progress remains on your device unless you clear the game's storage or uninstall it. Clearing local storage does not itself delete records already held by online services. An online identity deleted on request cannot be restored; choosing online play again may create a new identity.

Retention and your choices

You can continue playing offline without starting multiplayer services. Firebase availability checks may still occur. Local information remains until removed, overwritten or affected by backup settings. Online records follow the relevant service's retention practices. Support correspondence is retained as needed to fulfil requests and meet legitimate obligations. Contact us for access, correction or deletion requests.

Intended audience

The game is intended for ages 16 and above and is not directed to children under 16. Contact us if you believe a child supplied personal information so we can investigate. Store content ratings are determined separately and may vary by country.

Changes

We update this policy when the game or its services change. The revised date appears above. Where required, additional notice will be provided.
