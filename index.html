Privacy Policy

Bro City: Street Fight Game
Developer: ESK
Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Bro City: Street Fight Game, developed by ESK. For questions, contact developera44@gmail.com.

1. Game Information

The game stores progress, settings, virtual currency, unlocked content, purchase entitlements and transaction references on your device. The current game does not require an in-game account or provide its own cloud-save service. Clearing app data or uninstalling may remove local progress. Device backups may affect how this information is retained or restored.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The game integrates AppLovin MAX with advertising adapters for Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network and Unity Ads. Available advertising networks may vary.

These providers may process device and advertising identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, device and application information, ad interactions and diagnostics. This information may be used to deliver and measure ads, personalize advertising where permitted, prevent fraud and maintain or improve their services.

Processing depends on the services used, your region, device settings and applicable consent choices. Please review:

  • AppLovin Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Meta Privacy Policy
  • Unity Privacy Policy

3. In-App Purchases

Google Play processes Android payments. The game uses Unity In-App Purchasing, including receipts and transaction identifiers, to validate purchases, deliver virtual goods and restore eligible entitlements. We do not ask you to enter payment-card details inside the game. Google Play and Unity process information under their applicable policies.

4. Advertising Choices

Where required, advertising consent is requested through the configured consent service. Where supported, you can review consent choices in the game's settings. You can also manage advertising privacy controls in Android settings. Refusing personalized advertising does not necessarily remove all ads.

5. Support and Privacy Requests

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you provide. We use this information to respond to support and privacy requests and retain correspondence as needed to handle the inquiry and meet applicable obligations. Please do not send passwords or payment-card details.

You can remove local game data through Android's app settings. For information processed by advertising or payment providers, use their privacy controls and request channels. To request access, correction or deletion of information held by ESK, email developera44@gmail.com. Available rights depend on applicable law.

6. Retention and Security

Local game information remains until removed or overwritten, subject to platform backup behavior. Third-party providers apply their own retention practices and may process information in other countries. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Children

The game is intended for people aged 16 and over and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly request personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided personal information to ESK, please contact us so we can review and address the request. Store content ratings are determined separately and may vary by country.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent.
